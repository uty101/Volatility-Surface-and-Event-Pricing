# PLAN.md

Expanded from Section 8 of `instructions/00_kickoff.md`. One paragraph per step. Section and step numbers are the kickoff's; nothing is added or removed. Formulas, parameters and schemas are those of kickoff Sections 4 to 7 and are not restated. Items marked **OPEN O-n** are unspecified and listed in `decisions/OPEN.md`; they are not decided here, and the step builds everything that does not depend on them.

Standing for every step: every parameter comes from `config.toml`; every test runs under `pytest -p socket --disable-socket` and reads only `data/raw/`, `tests/fixtures/` or synthetic data; CSVs use `%.10g`, LF, UTF-8, no index column; review evidence is raw rows printed with `DataFrame.to_string()`.

Two items cut across sections. Where per-step driver code and helper functions that Section 6.1 does not list (for example a capture health builder) live is **OPEN O-1**. Output file names the kickoff does not fix are proposed below in `this style` and are **OPEN O-2** as a group; file names the kickoff fixes are used as written.
